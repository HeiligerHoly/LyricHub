# Third-party notices: lyrichub-recognize

The recognize plugin (`plugins/recognize`) is one program, `lyrichub-recognize.exe`, with ONNX
Runtime built in; it shares its audio and model code with the align plugin (`plugins/align`).
This file lists what it contains and what it downloads, with their licenses. The Rust part is
generated with `cargo about` (see `tools/licenses/README.md`); the other sections are kept by
hand in `tools/licenses/recognize.hbs`. The plugin itself is licensed like LyricHub, under MIT
with the Commons Clause (`LICENSE.txt` in the ZIP).

## ONNX Runtime 1.31.0

that the `ort` crate fetches (CPU; the DirectML provider is compiled in but its DLL is not
shipped or used). Its own third-party notices (protobuf, Abseil, Eigen, ONNX, FlatBuffers and
more) are in `onnxruntime-ThirdPartyNotices.txt` next to this file, unchanged from

## The model (downloaded, not shipped)

When the align plugin has not downloaded them already, the plugin downloads `aligner-int8.onnx` and `vocab.json` from
https://huggingface.co/redbaty/ytm-lyrics-aligner at revision
5a75dac46091289a9040ac88fca5cf288ea85699 and checks their SHA-256. They are an ONNX conversion of
mrfakename/w2v-bert-2.0-music-lyrics-ctc, a CTC model fine-tuned from Meta's
facebook/w2v-bert-2.0. All three are published under the MIT License; the training data of the
fine-tune is not documented.

The feature extraction (SeamlessM4T), the CTC alignment and the greedy CTC decoding are written in Rust after
transformers (Apache-2.0) and torchaudio (BSD-2-Clause); no code of either is shipped.
